EARLY SIGNALS

What we can take from this first snapshot

01

Preparedness is more than knowing a number

Four respondents felt very or somewhat prepared, while two reported low or no preparedness. Knowing how to contact emergency services does not automatically mean a household feels ready.

02

Clear, organized information matters

Emergency contact numbers were the most selected information need. Not knowing what to prepare and finding trustworthy information were the most frequently selected barriers.

03

A simple website is worth testing

Four respondents preferred a simple website. All six answered yes or maybe when asked about a free educational resource.

i
Methodology and limitations

This is a descriptive summary of six survey responses. Percentages are calculated from the displayed counts and rounded to one decimal place. The sample is small and may not represent the wider community. Results are preliminary and should not be generalized.
